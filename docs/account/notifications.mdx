Cloider tells you about your account in two places: the **Notifications** page in the dashboard, for announcements, and **email**, for things that need your attention, such as funds running low, a monthly invoice or a reply to a support request.

## At a glance

| Channel | Where | What it's for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Notifications page** | The bell icon in the dashboard's top bar | Announcements: new features, offers, budget warnings |
| **Email** | The email address of the account, or of the invited person | Balance warnings, suspension, invoices, invitations, support replies |

## The Notifications page

Click the bell icon in the top bar to open **Notifications**. When there is something new, the bell shows a count. Cloider uses notifications for new features, offers and sales, and warnings when you are about to exceed your budget.

When there is nothing new, the page says *You don't have any new notifications!*.

## Emails Cloider sends

| Email subject | Sent to | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Your Cloider account will be suspended in about 7 days* | The account Owner | 7 days before the account's funds run out at the current usage, and again 1 day before (*in about 1 day*), with the date |
| *Your Cloider account will be suspended on &lt;date&gt;* | The account Owner | When a credit expiring on that date would leave the account without funds |
| *Your Cloider account is suspended* | The account Owner | When the funds run out and the account is suspended. It gives the earliest date its resources can be removed |
| *Your Cloider resources will be removed on &lt;date&gt;* | The account Owner | Twice while the account is suspended, before its resources are removed |
| *Your Cloider resources were removed* | The account Owner | After a suspended account's resources are removed. The account and its balance are kept |
| *Your Cloider account is active again* | The account Owner | When funds are added to a suspended account and it is active again |
| *Your Cloider invoice for &lt;month&gt;* | The account Owner | Early each month, for the month before: what the usage came to, what paid for it, and the account balance over the month |
| *Invitation to Access a Cloider Account* | The invited email address | When an Owner or Admin invites someone. It names the account and asks the person to sign in with that email address |
| `Re: [#<request id>]: <subject>` | The account's email address | When the Cloider support team replies to a support request. The email links to the request in the dashboard |

The balance and suspension emails are explained in [What happens when your funds run out?](/account/billing#what-happens-when-your-funds-run-out). Emails from the support team can be answered from the dashboard: see [Support](/account/support).

:::note[Support replies go to the account's address]
Replies from the support team are emailed to the account's own address, the Owner's, even when a member opened the request. Members can follow every request of the account on the **Support** page.
:::

## Make sure you receive Cloider emails

- The account's email address is the one its Owner signs in with (for GitHub, the primary verified address). It can't be changed in Cloider.
- Add Cloider to your contacts or allow list so balance warnings don't land in spam.
- For a team account, sign up with a shared address the team reads (for example `ops@acme.com`), so balance warnings, invoices and support replies reach everyone who needs them.
