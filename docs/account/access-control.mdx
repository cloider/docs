**Access Control** is where the Owner and Admins decide who can reach the account and what they can do. You invite people by email address and give each one the **Admin** or **Restricted** role. This page walks through every member task.

If you are new to roles, read [Accounts and roles](/concepts/accounts-and-roles) first: it explains users, accounts and the full permission matrix.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Where** | Dashboard, **Access Control**, **Members** tab |
| **Who can manage members** | The Owner and Admins |
| **Roles you can give** | **Admin** (full access except billing) or **Restricted** (read-only) |
| **Member statuses** | **Pending**, **Active**, **Deactivated** |
| **Tools** | Dashboard only. The CLI, API and MCP can list members but not change them |

## Before you begin

- You need the **Owner** or **Admin** role in the account. Restricted members can only see the list and leave.
- Know the email address the person signs in to Google or GitHub with. The invitation is tied to that address.

## Invite a member

<Tabs groupId="interface" queryString>
<TabItem value="dashboard" label="Dashboard" default>

1. Open **Access Control** in the sidebar. The **Members** tab opens.
2. Click **Add New Member** (or **Invite member** if the account has no members yet).
3. In the **Invite member** dialog, type the person's **Email address**.
4. Pick an **Access role**: **Admin** or **Restricted**. **Information about access roles** explains the difference.
5. Click **Send Invitation**.

  caption="Inviting a member. The role can be changed later."
/>

The member appears in the list with the status **Pending**, and Cloider emails them an invitation titled *Invitation to Access a Cloider Account*.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="cli" label="CLI">

The CLI can't invite members. Members are managed in the dashboard only, so a leaked token can't hand out access. Use the **Dashboard** tab. To check the result:

```bash
cloider member list
```

```text title="Output"
EMAIL               ROLE        STATUS   NAME            ADDED
ops@acme.com        Owner       Active   Acme Inc        -
jane@acme.com       Admin       Active   -               2026-09-02 10:14
sam@acme.com        Restricted  Pending  -               2026-10-19 16:40
```

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="terraform" label="Terraform">

The Terraform provider doesn't manage members. Use the **Dashboard** tab.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="api" label="API">

`POST /api/members` only accepts a signed in dashboard session. With an API token it answers `403` with `This endpoint can't be used with an API token!`. You can list members with a token:

```bash
curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $CLOIDER_TOKEN" https://cloider.com/api/members
```

```json title="Response (trimmed)"
[
  {
    "email": "jane@acme.com",
    "role": "Admin",
    "status": "Active",
    "created_at": "2026-09-02T10:14:03Z",
    "updated_at": "2026-09-02T11:02:47Z"
  }
]
```

The Owner isn't a member row: `GET /api/whoami` returns the account itself.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="mcp" label="MCP">

The MCP server can't invite members. Ask your agent to list them instead:

```json title="Tool call: list_members"

```

### What the invited person does

1. They open the invitation email, or simply go to [cloider.com](https://cloider.com).
2. They sign in with **Google** or **GitHub**, using the invited email address. If they have no Cloider user yet, one is created.
3. The **Select an account** screen lists your account, highlighted as a pending invitation. They pick it.
4. The membership becomes **Active** and they land in your account's dashboard.

They can switch between their own account and yours at any time. See [Switch accounts](/account/switch-accounts).

## Change a member's role

1. In **Access Control**, **Members**, click the menu button on the member's row.
2. Click **Change role**.
3. In **Change member role**, pick the new **Access role** and click **Save**.

<Screenshot
  src="/img/screenshots/account/change-role.webp"
  url="/dashboard/access-control"
  alt="The Change member role dialog with the Access role selector open, offering Admin and Restricted"
  caption="Changing a member's role."
/>

The change applies to the member's next request. Changing someone to **Restricted** also switches all their API tokens in this account to **Read**.

## Deactivate or activate a member

**Deactivating** switches a member's access off without removing them, for example while someone is away.

1. Click the menu button on the member's row.
2. Click **Deactivate member**. The status becomes **Deactivated**.

A deactivated member can't open the account and their API tokens stop working. To restore access, open the same menu and click **Activate member**: the status goes back to **Active**, or to **Pending** if they never accepted the invitation.

## Remove a member

1. Click the menu button on the member's row.
2. Click **Remove member**, then confirm with **Remove**.

Removing a member ends their access immediately and **deletes the API tokens they created** in the account. Resources they created stay in the account: resources belong to the account, not to the person.

## Leave an account

As a member you can leave an account you were invited to:

1. Switch to that account, open **Access Control**, and click the menu button on **your own** row.
2. Click **Leave account**, then confirm with **Leave**.

You are taken back to the start, and the account disappears from your **Select an account** list. Your API tokens in that account are deleted. The Owner can't leave their own account.

## Verify

- The member's row shows the role and status you expect.
- The member sees the account on their **Select an account** screen, or in the account menu under **Switch account**.
- `cloider member list` lists them.

## Troubleshooting

| Message | Cause and fix |
| --- | --- |
| `Email address already added!` | That address is already a member (perhaps deactivated). Change its role or activate it instead. |
| `You can't add the account's email address!` | That is the Owner's address. The Owner doesn't need an invitation. |
| `Invalid email address!` | Check the address for typos. |
| `Role is required!` | Pick **Admin** or **Restricted** in **Access role**. |
| `You are not authorized to do this action!` | Your role is Restricted. Ask the Owner or an Admin. |
| The invited person doesn't see the account | They signed in with a different email address than the one you invited. Invite the address they actually use, or ask them to sign in with the other provider. |
| `This endpoint can't be used with an API token!` | Members can only be managed from the dashboard. |
