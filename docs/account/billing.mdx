Cloider is prepaid: your usage is paid from the account's funds as it happens, from its credits first and then its account balance. **Billing** shows those funds, how long they last at your current usage, this month's charges, your credits, and the monthly invoices and payments. Only the account **Owner** can open it.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Where** | Dashboard, **Billing** (in the sidebar for the Owner only). Every member sees the balance in the sidebar |
| **Who** | The account Owner. Admins and Restricted members see the balance and any warning, not the Billing page |
| **Funds** | The account balance plus active credits. Credits are spent first, the one that expires soonest first |
| **Adding funds** | **Add funds** on the Billing page. Support adds them for you until card payments are available |
| **When funds run out** | Clusters, databases and apps pause. After 14 days without a top-up, resources are removed |
| **Invoices** | One per month for the month before, with what paid for it and a statement of the account balance |
| **Tools** | Dashboard, `cloider wallet`, `cloider billing`, `GET /api/wallet`, `GET /api/billing`, the `get_wallet` and `get_billing` MCP tools |
| **How it's priced** | See [Billing model](/concepts/billing-model) |

## The Billing page

Open **Billing** in the sidebar. From top to bottom, the page shows:

- **Add funds**, at the top right, and a notice when something needs doing, such as funds running out soon.
- **Balance**: the funds available, split into **Account balance** and **Credits**, and how long they last at your current daily spend.
- **November so far**, named after the current month: this month's charges, the balance's opening amount and payments, the forecast for the month, last month's total, and the date of the next invoice. The bar under it shows how much credits and the balance paid.
- **Charges**: what was charged each day, by resource type, for the **Last 30 days** or **This month**.
- **If your balance runs out**: the three steps described in [What happens when your funds run out?](#what-happens-when-your-funds-run-out).
- **Credits**: every credit the account has had, with what is left of it and when it expires.
- **Payment methods**: card payments, coming soon.
- **Invoices** and **Payments** tabs, with a year filter and **Export CSV**.

## Add funds

Only the Owner adds funds, from the dashboard.

<Tabs groupId="interface" queryString>
<TabItem value="dashboard" label="Dashboard" default>

1. Open **Billing** and click **Add funds**, or click **Add funds** in the sidebar's balance card.
2. Pick an amount, or type one in **Amount**.
3. Click **Email support**. Your email app opens a message to support with the account and the amount.
4. Support replies with how to pay. Once the payment arrives, it is added to the account balance and listed under **Payments**.

Card payments, and topping up automatically before the balance runs out, are coming soon.

</TabItem>

A top-up pays what the account owes first, starting with the oldest invoice, and the rest is added to the balance. If the account was suspended, it is active again as soon as its funds are back above $1.

## Credits

A credit is an amount Cloider gives the account: the welcome credit of a new account, a promotion, a referral or a compensation. Credits pay for usage before the account balance does, the one that expires soonest first.

| Type | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Welcome credit** | $25 for a new account's 14-day trial, claimed from the dashboard. See [Create an account](/get-started/create-an-account#the-welcome-credit) |
| **Promo** | A promotion |
| **Referral** | A referral reward |
| **Compensation** | Compensation, for example after an incident |
| **Credit** | Any other credit |

Each credit has its own expiry date, or none. The **Credits** card shows each one's status:

- **Active**: it pays for usage until it is used up or expires.
- **Used**: it has been spent.
- **Expired**: it expired, and what was left of it is no longer available.

Credits pay for usage only. They don't pay off an amount the account balance owes, a top-up does.

## What happens when your funds run out?

When the account balance and the credits are used up, an account that has resources is **suspended**, and Cloider warns you before that happens:

1. **Warnings.** Cloider emails the Owner 7 days and 1 day before the funds run out at the current usage, and when a credit's expiry would leave the account without funds. A banner across the top of the dashboard and the sidebar's balance card say when, to every member.
2. **Suspension.** At zero, clusters, databases and apps are paused. Buckets and container registry projects keep working. The Owner gets an email with the earliest date the resources can be removed.
3. **Removal.** After 14 days suspended without a top-up, Cloider deletes the account's clusters, databases, apps, buckets and registry projects, with their data. Two warning emails come before that. Deleted resources can't be recovered.

While the account is suspended, storage is still charged: buckets, registry projects, and the volumes of paused databases and clusters, and a paused cluster's load balancers. That can take the balance below zero. A negative balance is an amount the account owes. It shows as **Payment due** and is paid first by the next top-up.

Removal keeps the account itself, its balance, credits, payments and invoices, its domains and DNS records, its members and its API tokens. [Targets](/domains/targets) that pointed at removed apps or buckets are deleted with them.

### Getting the account active again

[Add funds](#add-funds). As soon as the funds are back above $1, after anything the account owes, the account is active again and Cloider resumes the clusters, databases and apps the suspension paused. Resources you had paused yourself stay paused. The Owner gets an email, *Your Cloider account is active again*.

### What a suspended account can do

A suspended account, or one with no funds left, can't create or start anything that is charged for compute. These fail:

- creating a cluster, database, bucket, registry project or app,
- deploying an app, including redeploys, retries and a new image, and saving an app's settings, environment variables, build environment or build arguments,
- resuming a cluster, database or app, restoring a database backup and changing a database's plan.

A git push doesn't deploy an app while the account is suspended. Everything else keeps working: reading, pausing and deleting resources, buckets and the registry, domains, DNS records, targets, bucket keys, registry tokens, members, API tokens and support.

### Errors when funds run out

Requests that need funds fail with HTTP `402` and one of these messages:

| Message | Cause |
| --- | --- |
| `Your account is suspended because its credit ran out. Top up to continue.` | The account is suspended. [Add funds](#add-funds) |
| `Your account has no credit left. Top up to continue.` | The account has no funds left, and isn't suspended yet. [Add funds](#add-funds) |
| `Claim your $25 welcome credit from the dashboard to start your trial.` | A new account that hasn't claimed its welcome credit. Click **Start trial** in the dashboard |

## View your invoices

Cloider issues an invoice for each month the account had usage, early in the next month, and emails it to the Owner. Since usage is paid as it happens, an invoice is mostly a record: it lists what the month cost, what paid for it, and how the account balance changed.

<Tabs groupId="interface" queryString>
<TabItem value="dashboard" label="Dashboard" default>

1. Open **Billing** in the sidebar and choose the **Invoices** tab. Each invoice shows its **Month**, **Amount**, what it was **Paid by** and its **Status**. Pick a year to see only that year's.
2. Click an invoice to open it. The dialog lists every resource with its lines, the **Payment sources** and the **Statement** of the account balance.
3. Click **PDF** on an invoice's row, or the printer icon (**Print**) in the dialog, to print it or save it as a PDF from your browser's print dialog.

**Export CSV** downloads the invoices shown, with their amount, what paid for them, their status, the amount due and what is left to pay.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="cli" label="CLI">

```bash
cloider billing
```

```text title="Output"

October 2026   $61.80   1 credit + account balance   Paid
```

## What an invoice contains

An invoice has one **item** per resource that cost something that month. Each item has **lines** (subitems) for what was metered:

| Item | Lines | Unit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Cluster: "<name>"` | **Compute** (average CPU and memory, usage time), **Persistent Volumes** (average GB), one **LoadBalancer** line per load balancer (its name and IP) | Compute-Hour, GB-Hour, Hour |
| `App: "<name>"` | **Compute** | Compute-Hour |
| `Database: "<name>"` | The engine (for example **PostgreSQL**) with its plan and monthly price, and **Persistent Volume** for time spent paused | Hour, GB-Hour |
| `Bucket: "<name>"` | **Object Storage** (average GB stored) | GB-Hour |
| `Registry project: "<name>"` | **Container Registry** (average GB stored) | GB-Hour |

The invoice dialog's columns are:

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Service** | What the line is for. When prices changed during the month, the line names its price period |
| **Description** | Detail such as `Avg. CPU usage: 0.84 cores`, `Avg usage: 20 GB` or `Plan: Starter ($15/mo)` |
| **Usage** | The quantity, in the line's unit |
| **Unit** | **Compute-Hour**, **GB-Hour** or **Hour** |
| **Rate** | The hourly rate: the monthly price divided by 720 |
| **Cost** | Usage times rate |

Items with several lines end with a **Subtotal**, and the invoice ends with **Total Amount**.

- A **Compute-Hour** is one compute unit for one hour. See [compute units](/concepts/billing-model#compute-units).
- A **GB-Hour** is one GB stored or provisioned for one hour. 20 GB for a whole 720 hour month is 14,400 GB-Hours.
- If a database changed plan during the month, it has one line per plan, each with the dates it covers.

Under the items, the invoice shows:

- **Payment sources**: what paid for the month's usage, each credit by name and the **Account balance**. **Left owed** is usage the balance couldn't pay, and **Paid later** is the part of it that top-ups paid since. Invoices from before prepaid billing can show **Paid directly** or **Waived**.
- **Statement** (account balance): the **Opening balance** on the first of the month, the **Payments** during it (top-ups less refunds), the usage **Charged to balance**, and the **Closing balance**. When the closing balance is below zero, **Amount due** is what the account owed at the end of the month.

## Invoice status

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Paid** | Nothing is owed on it. When it was paid by a later top-up, the date follows: *Paid on Dec 3, 2026* |
| **Due** | The account still owes part of it, shown as *$12.40 left to pay*. [Add funds](#add-funds) to pay it |
| **Carried over** | An older invoice still owed. What it owes is included in the amount due of a later invoice, named as *to the December 2026 statement* |
| **Waived** | Nothing to pay |

The amount due of an invoice includes earlier months still owed, shown as *Includes $8.20 carried from earlier months*. Don't add amounts due across invoices: the latest **Due** invoice shows everything the account owes.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause and fix |
| --- | --- |
| No **Billing** in the sidebar | You aren't the account Owner. Only the Owner sees the Billing page. The sidebar's balance card shows the funds to everyone. |
| `You don't have access to this account billing page!` or `You don't have access to this account's wallet!` | The request came from an Admin, a Restricted member, or a token that isn't the Owner's. Invoices also need a **Read & Write** token. |
| `Claim your $25 welcome credit from the dashboard to start your trial.` | A new account hasn't claimed its welcome credit. Click **Start trial** in the prompt at the top of the dashboard. |
| `Your account has no credit left. Top up to continue.` | The funds are used up. [Add funds](#add-funds). |
| `Your account is suspended because its credit ran out. Top up to continue.` | The account is suspended. [Add funds](#add-funds), and it is active again once its funds are back above $1. |
| The balance is below zero | Storage kept being charged while the account was suspended. The next top-up pays it first. |
| An amount looks wrong | Compare it with the resource's daily figures on [Usage](/account/usage), then open a support request with the type **Billing/Payment/Pricing Question or Issue**. |
